About NES Playlog

Your checkmarks, ratings, favorites, dates, notes, and achievement badges are stored in this browser using localStorage. Game progress is not sent to a server. When online box art is enabled, your browser requests cover thumbnails from a third-party community archive.

The archive includes a growing set of NES, Famicom and Disk System game records, including a clearly marked unreleased NES prototype. This is an expanding and carefully checked selection, not yet the complete worldwide catalog. Famicom Disk System belongs to the Famicom family, not the SNES.

Alternate cartridge titles like Contra / Probotector share one game record and one play status. Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels remains separate from Super Mario Bros. 2, since they are different games.

Two appearance themes are available: NES in grey, charcoal and red, and Famicom in ivory and burgundy. Artwork is optional, loaded from an external thumbnail catalog where available and shown with a built-in fallback when unavailable or offline. Box art remains copyright of its respective owners.

Opening the standalone file and visiting an online version use different storage. Player passports are private to each browser, and no login or cloud sync is required. Games can have multiple genre and gameplay tags, and creator metadata is still growing. Export a backup here and import it on the new site. Clearing browser data can erase your progress.

Your previous v1, v2, v3, v4 and v5 NES Playlog saves and backups are supported. Using a new domain or standalone file needs export and import. If you added an alternate cartridge title as a separate custom game, known aliases are consolidated while preserving the strongest progress and notes.
